# FigJam workspace interaction references

Captured by the product owner on 2026-08-12 and retained as visual direction for **Milestone 2 — Workspace experience and interaction system**.

These screenshots are reference evidence for spatial clarity, progressive disclosure, contextual controls, compact application chrome, and panel behavior. They do not authorize a pixel-for-pixel reproduction of FigJam or use of Figma branding. Thinking Canvas must retain its own visual identity and meet the requirements and exit gate in the master implementation plan.

## Reference set

1. [`01-workspace-overview.png`](01-workspace-overview.png) — Full-bleed canvas, compact corner controls, floating tool dock, and minimal navigation controls.
2. [`02-drawing-tool-palette.png`](02-drawing-tool-palette.png) — A drawing tool family with a secondary palette for tool and color choices.
3. [`03-shape-tool-palette.png`](03-shape-tool-palette.png) — Progressive disclosure of connector and shape variants above the primary dock.
4. [`04-comments-panel.png`](04-comments-panel.png) — A dismissible contextual side panel that overlays the canvas instead of permanently shrinking the workspace.
5. [`05-selection-formatting-toolbar.png`](05-selection-formatting-toolbar.png) — A selection-sensitive floating toolbar with applicable formatting actions.
6. [`06-fill-color-popover.png`](06-fill-color-popover.png) — A contextual fill-mode and color popover anchored to the selection toolbar.
7. [`07-sticky-note-color-palette.png`](07-sticky-note-color-palette.png) — A compact contextual palette for an active creation tool.

## Principles to carry into Thinking Canvas

- Keep the canvas visually primary.
- Reveal detailed controls in response to the active tool, selection, or opened panel.
- Keep frequent actions close to their object or tool context.
- Reserve permanent chrome for identity, collaboration, primary tools, and navigation.
- Preserve keyboard access, touch-sized targets, visible state, and non-color-only feedback.
- Use original Thinking Canvas tokens, components, language, and branding.
